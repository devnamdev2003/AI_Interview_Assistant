Here's a professional email template design for each of these scenarios. Each template can be adapted to fit your application's branding and style.

---

### 1. Email Verification

**Subject:** Verify Your Email for [Application Name]

**Body:**

```
Hi [User Name],

Thank you for signing up for [Application Name]! To complete your registration, please verify your email by clicking the link below:

[Verification Link]

This helps us ensure the security of your account. If you did not sign up for [Application Name], please ignore this email.

Best Regards,  
The [Application Name] Team

*Need help? Contact our support team at [support email].*
```

---

### 2. Password Reset

**Subject:** Password Reset Request for Your [Application Name] Account

**Body:**

```
Hi [User Name],

We received a request to reset your password for your [Application Name] account. To proceed with resetting your password, please click on the link below:

[Password Reset Link]

If you did not request this change, please ignore this email. Your password will remain the same.

Best Regards,  
The [Application Name] Team

*For any assistance, reach out to us at [support email].*
```

---

### 3. Account Deletion

**Subject:** Account Deletion Confirmation for [Application Name]

**Body:**

```
Hi [User Name],

We're sorry to see you go. Your account has been successfully deleted from [Application Name]. We hope that your experience was positive, and we would welcome any feedback you may have.

Should you wish to return, you're always welcome to create a new account with us.

Best Regards,  
The [Application Name] Team

*If you have questions or need further assistance, contact us at [support email].*
```

---

### 4. Welcome Email (Account Creation)

**Subject:** Welcome to [Application Name], [User Name]!

**Body:**

```
Hi [User Name],

Welcome to [Application Name]! We’re thrilled to have you on board. Our goal is to make your experience enjoyable and rewarding. Get started by exploring the platform and customizing your settings.

If you have any questions or need assistance, our support team is just a click away.

Happy Exploring,  
The [Application Name] Team

*Need help? Feel free to reach out at [support email].*
```

---

### Additional Styling Tips

For a more polished look:
- Use a header image or banner representing your brand.
- Add a signature logo or branding colors.
- Structure the email with sections, such as "Need Help?", "Contact Us", or "Follow Us on Social Media."
